TITLE: Agriculture, Forestry and Other Land Use
SOURCE: Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC), AR6 WGIII Chapter 7
SOURCE_URL: https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg3/chapter/chapter-7/

The IPCC identifies agriculture, forestry and other land use as an area where land-management measures can contribute to climate mitigation while also providing food, materials and biodiversity-related benefits.

The IPCC emphasizes that land-based measures can have co-benefits but may also involve trade-offs. Outcomes depend on how measures are implemented and on interactions with food production, habitats, adaptation and biodiversity.

KEY VARIABLES:
- Land management
- Land use
- Biodiversity
- Habitat conservation
- Climate
- Carbon
- Food production

RELEVANCE:
Environmental recommendations should consider interactions among multiple objectives rather than assuming that one intervention always produces the same outcome. Site conditions and implementation choices affect both benefits and trade-offs.
